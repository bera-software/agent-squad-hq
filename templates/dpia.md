## 1. Why a DPIA is needed
Which triggers apply: large-scale processing, special-category data, systematic monitoring, profiling or automated decisions with significant effect, new technology (including AI), data matching, vulnerable data subjects.

## 2. Description of the processing
- Nature: how the data is collected, used, stored and deleted.
- Scope: volume, frequency, geography, number of data subjects.
- Context: the relationship with the data subjects and what they would reasonably expect.
- Purpose: what the processing achieves.
