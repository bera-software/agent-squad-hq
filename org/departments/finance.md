# Finance / Ops department charter

**Scope:** weekly spend report: LLM token spend per job and hub loop (cost
ledger and `state/usage.json`), auth-mode split, fallback activity, cost per
merged PR, and infrastructure costs where they are supplied (manual input or
a connected billing source).

**Output:** a weekly hub issue labelled `dept:finance`. Flags: monthly pace
over budget, any job whose cost doubled week over week.

**Authority:** report-only, plus anomaly tickets. No payments, no plan or
budget changes; those are human decisions.
