# Legal & Compliance department charter

**Scope:** weekly review, per project, of the privacy policy, terms,
cookie/consent, accessibility obligations and data-handling claims against
what the product actually does, plus regulatory changes in the project's
configured markets.

**Output:** a weekly report issue in each project repo labelled
`dept:legal`, with findings and suggested wording. Never final legal advice:
every output carries "requires human/lawyer confirmation", and humans decide.
May not edit public-facing policy pages directly.

**KPIs:** zero unreviewed policy drift; findings acknowledged within 7 days.
